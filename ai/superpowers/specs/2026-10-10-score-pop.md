# MY-124 — Score pop: a "+N" over the score after a move (spec)

Issue: MY-124 · Branch: `my-124-score-pop` · Plan: `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-score-pop.md`

## Goal

A move that merges tiles shows how many points it brought: a short `+4`, `+16`, `+128`
appears next to the score, rises a little, and fades out. Every merge feels like it
counts. The score itself, the header, and what a screen reader says do not change.

## What is wrong today

- The score only changes its number. After a move it is not obvious how much the move
  was worth, especially in a burst of fast moves or when a big merge brings several
  tiles together at once.

## Behavior

### When the pop shows

- After a move that earned points, a `+N` appears, where `N` is the sum of all merges in
  that move: the same number that was just added to the score (`2 + 2` and `4 + 4` in one
  move give `+12`).
- A move that only slides tiles (no merge) shows nothing.
- A move that changes nothing (blocked, during the 100 ms cooldown, after game over, with
  a dialog open) is ignored as before and shows nothing.
- `New Game`, `Restart Game`, and a game restored from the save after a reload show
  nothing: they are not moves. The page loads with no pop.
- The move that wins or ends the game still shows its pop; it sits under the dimmed dialog
  backdrop, next to the score that already changed.

### What it looks like

- Text `+N` in the gold of the score digits, bold, a little smaller than the score, in the
  `Score` box: at its right edge, level with the score value. It starts at full opacity
  where the value is, rises about 28px toward the label row and fades out, in about 600 ms.
  It is gone from the page when it has faded.
- It never moves or resizes anything: it floats over the box (out of the layout), so the
  header does not shift when a pop appears, grows, or goes away, at any width from 320px
  up. It does not block taps or clicks under it.
- The number is the plain integer, like the score itself (`+1024`, no thousands
  separator).

### A burst of moves

- At most one pop is on screen. A move that earns points while the previous pop is still
  in the air replaces it with the new one, which starts at once. The player always sees
  the points of the last move, never a pile of numbers drawn over one another.
- Every pop is its own element with its own animation and is removed from the DOM when
  that animation ends; a replaced pop is removed at once. After the last pop has faded
  nothing is left behind in the DOM.
- The pop of a move never delays or swallows the next move: the animation is only a
  decoration.

### Reduced motion

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the `+N` still shows, but without movement: it
appears in place, next to the score value, and fades out there (about the same 600 ms).
It does not rise. (Author's decision, 2026-10-10.)

### Screen readers

- The pop is `aria-hidden="true"`: it is not in the accessibility tree, it is not inside
  the score's live region, and it is not part of the visible score text.
- The score is announced exactly as before (`ai/decisions/2026-10-10-score-live-region-and-focus-ring.md`):
  the bare new number, once, 500 ms after the last change. `+N` is never read.

### Unchanged

- Moves, scoring, high score, the saved game, the dialogs and their focus handling, the
  live region and its debounce, the tile animations (slides are MY-126).
- The score box, the high score box, and the toolbar look and size exactly as before when
  no pop is showing.

## How it will be checked

No automated tests exist. The change is played in `yarn dev`, driven by a headless Chrome
over the DevTools protocol where the built-in browser pane cannot run animations:

- A move with a merge shows the right `+N`; a slide, `New Game`, and a restored save show
  nothing.
- Fast key presses (the 100 ms cooldown rhythm): one pop at a time, and an empty
  container once it ends.
- Swipes at 320px: the header boxes keep their exact size and position with a pop in the
  air.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` emulated: the pop shows and fades with no vertical
  movement.
- The accessibility tree has no `+N`, and the live region text changes only to the bare
  score, once.
