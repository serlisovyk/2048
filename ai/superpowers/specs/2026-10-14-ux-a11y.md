# MY-140 — UX and accessibility: the board fits the window, contrast, 44px targets, a named board, trash icons (spec)

Issue: MY-140 · Branch: `my-140-ux-a11y` · Plan: `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-ux-a11y.md`

Source: the UX audit of 2026-10-13 (production build, 1440–320px, both themes). Focus, `Escape`, reduced motion and
the heading levels were fine; the board did not fit the window height on laptops and in landscape, some text and tiles
were under 4.5:1, some targets under 44px, and the board had no name a screen reader could use.

## Author's decisions (2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14, final)

- The high score reset becomes a **trash** icon. The goal stays **off** the screen: it is only in the board's accessible
  name. The game-over card is made **opaque**, nothing else changes there. **No thousands separators** (a 7-digit score
  does not fit at 320px).
- **Statistics as 2×2 tiles** on a tinted backing, the reset a trash icon in the heading row (of three mock-ups: tiles,
  rows, strip). The settings title gets lighter (~28px) and its section headings larger (~20px). A small muted line
  **All board sizes** under the "Statistics" heading; short tile labels: Games played, Wins, Best score, Best tile.
- The board grows on big screens up to **~600px** (a ceiling, so tiles do not become huge); the header and the footer grow
  with it. The near-duplicate tokens of MY-139 are merged: the three 14–18px font sizes, and the shadow blurs 8 / 12 / 15px.
- Not here (MY-141): button and dialog texts, the settings dialog's behaviour and first focus, `SettingsView` /
  `SettingsController`, swipe and keyboard input. The statistics reset keeps its hook class `.reset-statistics-button`.

## The board fits the window (item 1)

- The cell size follows the width **and** the height of the window. At 1366×657, 1280×600 and 667×375 the whole board is
  visible with no page scroll on 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5; at 1440×900, 1000×600, 768×1024, 375×812 and 320×568 it is as big as
  before or bigger. The GitHub row stays.
- A cell is never smaller than **40px** because of the height (a window too short for that scrolls, as before). It is
  never wider than the window allows: no horizontal scroll from 320px up.
- On a big screen a 4×4 or 5×5 board is about **600px** wide at most; a 3×3 board keeps the 4×4 cell, so it never gets bigger
  tiles than 4×4. The header and the footer are as wide as the board at every size, as before.
- **A short landscape window** (up to 570px tall, at least 540px wide, wider than tall: a phone on its side) cannot hold the
  header, the board and the footer in one column with cells of 40px. There the header (logo, toolbar, scores) and the footer sit in a
  column **beside** the board; their content and order do not change, and the GitHub row is still the top row.
- Nothing else about the board changes: the tile text is still a share of the cell, no pixel floor.

## Contrast (item 2)

- The line "You've reached 2048!" in the win dialog is at full opacity and reaches **4.5:1** everywhere on the card: the
  light end of the card's gradient is darkened. The summary and button texts on it get better with it.
- Tile text reaches **4.5:1** against both ends of the tile's gradient, for every tile (the cell can be 40px, so the text
  can be 14px). Only the tokens that fail get darker or lighter: 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048 (the ramp is kept in order with
  the same spacing between neighbours) and 8192, 16384. Both themes: the tile colours are the same in both.
- The favicon's 2048 tile and the link preview picture follow the new tokens (`ai/skills/og-image.md`).

## Touch targets and icons (items 3, 4)

- Every dialog button is at least **44px** high; the segments already are. The high score reset has a **44×44px** hit area
  around a 24px icon.
- The high score reset is a **trash** icon (24px inline SVG, like the others, round hover highlight). Its focus ring stays
  clear of the score box's border. It is still hidden while the record is 0, still asks first, behaviour unchanged.

## The dialogs (items 5, 9, 10)

- The game-over card is opaque; one button, behaviour unchanged.
- The **confirmation** and the **settings** titles are ~28px; the settings section headings ~20px. The win and game-over
  titles stay big (40px).
- **One label style over a number**: uppercase (by CSS, the markup stays sentence case), bold, muted, small. It is the
  header's "Score" label, the game summary's labels (Score, Moves, Highest tile) and the statistics' labels.
- **Statistics**: under the heading a muted line "All board sizes"; at the right end of the heading row the trash icon
  button, `aria-label` and `title` "Reset statistics", the same component as the high score reset, hidden while all four
  numbers are 0; below, four tiles in 2×2 (Games played, Wins, Best score, Best tile), each a label over the number on a tinted
  backing (the game summary's tint in dark cards; a light tint in the light settings). The question that follows the
  button (the settings close, the confirmation opens, the settings reopen on "no") is unchanged. The text button
  "Reset statistics" is gone.
- The **New record!** badge keeps clear of the settings button at 320–375px (and wider): a visible gap, no overlap.

## Screen readers and keyboard (items 6, 7, 8)

- The board is `role="group"` named **`2048 board, 4×4, goal 2048`** (size and win target of the game on it). The name
  changes with a new size or target. The goal is not on screen.
- The score is announced as **`Score 16`**, and a record as **`New record! Score 104`** (the debounce and the rules for
  when it speaks do not change). The visible "Score" label is hidden from assistive technology so browsing the header does
  not say "Score" twice.
- The GitHub link is **not the first `Tab` stop**: it comes after the game in the DOM and `Tab` order (New game, theme,
  settings, the reset when shown, then the link); on screen it stays in the top-right row.

## Tokens

- `--font-control`, `--font-text` and `--font-pop` become one step; the shadow blurs 8, 12 and 15px become fewer steps. The look
  is checked at 375–720px; no other size, radius or duration changes. Two new font tokens for the new headings.

## Unchanged

Game rules, saves, settings and their texts, the statistics' meaning, swipe and keyboard input, dialog behaviour and
focus, animations and reduced motion, the theme.

## How it will be checked

`yarn lint`, `yarn format:check`, `yarn build`, `yarn size`; then the production build in a browser at 1440×900,
1366×657, 1280×600, 1000×600, 768×1024, 375×812, 320×568 and 667×375, both themes, reduced motion, the keyboard
(`Tab` order, rings), the accessibility tree (board name, live-region text), contrast of the changed texts and tiles by
pixels, the targets by hit-testing; and a game played: moves, win, game over, new game, settings, the statistics reset,
the high score reset. Not checkable here: a real screen reader, Safari, Firefox.
