# MY-135 — Footer with the author's name and the year, and a GitHub link at the top right (spec)

Issue: MY-135 · Branch: `my-135-footer` · Plan: `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-10-13-footer.md`

## Goal

The page says nowhere who made it or where the code is. For a portfolio piece both should be
visible without being in the way: a quiet footer line with the name and the year, and a small
GitHub icon in the top right corner of the page that opens the repository.

## What is wrong today

- Nothing on the page names the author.
- Nothing links to the source code.

## Behavior

### Footer

- The text `Serhii Lisovyk · <year>`, for example `Serhii Lisovyk · 2026`. It is plain text: no
  link, no button.
- The year is the current year of the visitor's clock, filled in when the page loads. It is not
  written in the markup, so it never goes stale; the markup holds only the name.
- Above the text, a thin divider line as wide as the board. The text is centred under it, in the
  muted text colour at a small size.
- Both colours (line and text) come from tokens and read in the dark and in the light theme.
- The footer is the page's `contentinfo` landmark. It is not focusable and holds nothing a
  keyboard needs.

### GitHub icon

- A link in the very top right of the page, a 44x44px target (the size of the toolbar buttons)
  holding the GitHub mark as inline SVG (24px, `currentColor`, `aria-hidden`, `focusable="false"`).
- `href="https://github.com/serlisovyk/2048"`, `target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener"`. It has an
  `aria-label` and a `title` with the same text: `Source code on GitHub (opens in a new tab)`.
- It is **not** in the header's toolbar: the toolbar keeps its three buttons. It has a row of its
  own above the header, so at no width does it share a line with, or cover, the logo, the toolbar,
  the score boxes or the board.
- Quiet by default (the muted icon colour), the strong icon colour and a soft round highlight on
  hover. On keyboard focus it shows the same focus ring as every button (`Tab` shows it, a click
  does not).
- Colours through tokens, both themes.
- It is the first thing in the page in reading and `Tab` order, which is also its visual place
  (the top row). One `Tab` from the page start reaches it, and the next goes on to `New game`.

### Layout

- The page is one column: the icon row, then the game (header and board), then the footer.
- **There is room** (the viewport is taller than the content): the icon is at the top, the footer is
  at the bottom of the viewport, and the header and board sit in the space between them with equal
  space above and below, so they are about where they were before (within a few pixels, not moved
  sideways at all).
- **There is no room**: the icon row is the first thing at the top, the footer follows the board at
  the usual gap, and the page scrolls vertically by the missing amount. The footer is never over
  the board and is never cut off.
- At 320x568 the whole game, the icon row and the footer fit without vertical scrolling for the
  3x3, 4x4 and 5x5 boards (the plan has the measured numbers). Shorter screens scroll a little: the
  icon row and the footer add about 90px of height.
- No horizontal scroll at any width from 320px up. The footer line is as wide as the board at every
  width and size: it never widens the board's wrapper (the same rule as the header).
- The icon respects the safe-area insets like the rest of the page (the body padding).

### Dialogs

- The icon and the footer sit outside every `<dialog>`, so while a modal dialog is open they are
  behind its backdrop and inert (not focusable, not clickable), like the rest of the page.

### Unchanged

- Game rules, the saved game, the settings, the dialogs and their texts, the board and the header
  (their size, their width and their contents), keyboard and swipe input.
- The toolbar still holds three buttons and fits at 320px as before.

## How it will be checked

`yarn lint`, `yarn format:check`, `yarn build`, `yarn size`, then play it in `yarn dev` (port 5191)
in a real browser:

- 1000 / 768 / 375 / 320px (and 320x568), the three board sizes, both themes: the icon at the top
  right, the footer at the bottom or after the board, nothing overlapping (rectangles compared),
  no horizontal overflow, the board and the header at the same x and the same width as before;
- the position of the board with room, before and after, and what happens when there is none;
- `Tab` from the page start: the GitHub icon first, then the toolbar; the focus ring on the icon,
  none after a click;
- the link opens `https://github.com/serlisovyk/2048` in a new tab (the attributes are checked, the
  site itself is not opened);
- a dialog open (win, game over, the confirmation, the settings): the icon is not focusable and the
  footer is behind the backdrop;
- the year changes with the clock (the fake clock of the browser);
- contrast of every new colour pair in both themes, by the WCAG formula.

What cannot be checked here: a screen reader's actual speech and Safari or Firefox rendering. The
accessibility tree and the DOM are checked instead.
